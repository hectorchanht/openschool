What it is呢個係咩

All the Open* products get one front door: OpenSchool. It is the shelves and the hub; OpenTeacher is its guide and teaching engine. Its seed foundation is the vault — Dawn's canon, shelved and growing.

The doctrine is ship at the beginning: the door opens with whatever canon exists; the pipeline keeps filling and updating it forever. No “launch when complete” — completeness is a process, not a gate.

And the measure is the feeling itself. After the founding all-night conversation, the founder named what the night felt like — the joy of continuously gaining knowledge. That feeling IS OpenSchool's product: every user should feel exactly this. The school's success metric is this feeling's presence, not course counts. The path: the founder walks it first — then it ships to the whole world.

所有 Open* 產品得一個大門：OpenSchool。佢係書架同樞紐；OpenTeacher 係佢嘅嚮導同教學引擎。佢嘅種子根基係 vault — Dawn 嘅典籍，上咗架，而且keep住長大。

宗旨係一開始就出街（ship at the beginning）：大門用現有嘅典籍開門；管線永遠keep住填、keep住更新。冇「完成先 launch」— 完成係一個過程，唔係一道閘。

而量度嘅，就係嗰種感覺本身。創校通宵長談之後，創辦人講出嗰晚嘅感覺 — 持續獲得知識嘅快樂。嗰種感覺就係 OpenSchool 嘅產品：每個用戶都應該感受到。學校嘅成功指標係呢種感覺在唔在場，唔係課程數量。路徑：由創辦人行先 — 然之後推俾全世界。
